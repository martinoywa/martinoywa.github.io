Hi, I’m **Martin Oywa**, a Machine Learning Engineer passionate about building AI systems that bridge research and real-world impact. I specialize in **Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing**, with growing expertise in **MLOps and Generative AI**.

I’m currently pursuing a **Master’s in Computer Science**, where my research explores whether **timbre transfer of the human singing voice can help improve automatic music transcription of singing**. This work combines my interests in audio processing, deep learning, and most importantly **creative AI applications**.

I’ve worked on projects ranging from **LLM-powered applications** to **custom image and audio models**, always with a focus on making solutions efficient, scalable, and user-friendly.

Beyond engineering, I’m motivated by a bigger vision: to establish an **AI research hub in Kenya**, enabling local talent to contribute to global advancements in artificial intelligence.
